## What stands

`scenarios/storefront` runs through a landed bolt, stepped from the page:
`scripts/tour-walk.sh` applies it, serves it, clicks `next` nineteen times
and screenshots every step (`target/shots/tour/`). Step 17 shows the chore
unit in its stage with a session alive, 18 the bolt asking to close with its
mark, 19 the landed record. Both runners play it; the harness system test
`storefront_runs_through_its_authored_actions` is green.

The toolchain is devenv's (`devenv shell -- unit|integration|system`); every
cargo command runs inside it. The unit tier is 4.5 s of tests. Do not run
the integration tier while building; the pre-merge hook runs it.
