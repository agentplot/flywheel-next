# Citing a claim from construction

Cite the standing claim in scope the work serves. It is already among the
inputs; nothing else is in scope, and nothing outside them is to be looked
for.

Cite none when none fits. Work that names no claim satisfies none, produces
no as-built statement and no verdict, and lands like any other work. That is
a normal outcome and it is stated plainly in the exit, never papered over by
citing a claim the work does not actually serve.

Never propose a claim for this work. Claims are written on the design side.
Small work connects to the destination by citing a coarse claim, not by
adding a fine one.

Name the claim and its version in every as-built statement written, so the
built repository says what it serves and the judgment has something to read
(99).
