# Writing the fundamentals part

Every clause is an invariant: something always true or never done. Not a
plan, not a feature, not a preference. If it could stop being true next
quarter without anything being wrong, it is not a clause.

Numbering is one sequence across the part. A new clause takes the next free
number. No clause is ever renumbered and no number is ever reused, because
everything that cites one cites it by number.

Name no mechanism. No store, no service, no format, no tool, no path, no
field. A clause says what must hold in the language a person judges it in;
the mechanism that makes it hold belongs in a chapter.

A clause that the flywheel can check per repository has its claim included by
anchor immediately after it. A clause need not yield one. A clause that
constrains only how the model is built yields none, and the exit says so
rather than inventing one.
