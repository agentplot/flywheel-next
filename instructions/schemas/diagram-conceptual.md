# A conceptual diagram

One picture of what the parts are and how they relate, for a reader who does
not yet know the system.

It shows things a person in the domain would name, and nothing a person in the
domain would not: no process, no queue, no file, no class. Every shape is
labelled with the word the book uses for it, spelled the same way.

The house style is fixed here so that two diagrams drawn a year apart look
like one system: the palette, the shape per kind of thing, the direction of
flow, and the rule that a label is a noun phrase.

Invalid: a diagram that introduces a word the book does not use, an unlabelled
edge, more than one kind of relationship drawn the same way, and a picture
that cannot be read at the width the book renders it.
