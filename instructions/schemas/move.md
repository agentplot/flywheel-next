# A move

Curation's stored judgment on one signal. Exactly one per signal, and once
stored it stands.

It names the signal, the judgment — attach to an open intent, challenge a
standing claim, join a proposed new intent, answered by a decision made since,
route to a chore or an ask, or drop — its object where the judgment has one,
and the reason in a sentence a stranger could weigh.

"Drop" is a real judgment and takes a real reason. So does "answered": it
names the decision that answered it.

Invalid: a move with no reason, a move naming a claim or an intent that does
not exist, two moves on one signal, and a move that acts on the world rather
than recording a judgment about it.
