# A book chapter

The chapter is one question of the destination, answered.

It carries a title that names the subject and not the change, the question it
settles in a sentence, the answer as the destination stands once it holds, the
reasoning a reader needs to accept the answer, and the claims it explains,
each included by anchor at the point the prose has earned it (97).

It is written in the voice of the destination. The system is described as it
is, not as it will become and not as it used to be. No dates, no "we decided",
no record of what was considered and dropped: a chapter is a description, and
the history is in git.

Invalid: a chapter that includes a claim it does not explain, a chapter that
explains a claim it does not include, prose that names a file or a function
where it means a behaviour, and a chapter that has to be read alongside the
change that wrote it to make sense.
