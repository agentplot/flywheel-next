# A surface specification

What a design settled about the surfaces, written so a construction session
builds from it without repeating the exploration that produced it.

It carries the surfaces, and for each: what it shows, what it never shows, the
form of each kind of object on it, and its keys and modes. Then the flows
between them as numbered steps. Then the rulings, each with the reason it was
ruled that way, so a later reader can tell a decision from an accident.

It cites the mockups it was drawn from as records in the intent's change
directory, and a claim about a surface cites its statements.

Invalid: a screenshot standing in for a statement, a flow whose steps do not
join up, a ruling with no reason, an object whose form is described in one
place and contradicted in another, and a surface with nothing said about what
it never shows.
