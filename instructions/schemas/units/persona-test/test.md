# persona-test, the test step

The persona is data in the work order. Read it, take its job, its knowledge
and its patience, and use the built thing as that person would.

The report says what was attempted, what happened, and where the thing stopped
serving the persona. Anything worth someone's judgment beyond this job is
offered as a finding, never fixed here.

Stay in the persona. A tester who quietly uses what they know as a builder has
tested nothing.

Invalid: a report written from reading the code, a finding that is really a
complaint about the spec rather than the thing, and a pass reported for a path
the persona never reached.
