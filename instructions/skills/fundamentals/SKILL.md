# Writing the fundamentals part

The part states what the system is and what must always hold. Nothing else
belongs in it.

Read the part as it stands, with its clause numbers, before writing. A new
clause takes the next free number; no existing clause is renumbered, because
everything that cites a clause cites its number.

Each clause is an invariant — something always true or never done — in the
language a person judges it in, naming no mechanism. Where a clause can be
checked per repository, write the claim that checks it and include it by
anchor immediately after the clause. A clause that constrains only how the
model is built yields no claim, and the exit says so.

The session is done when the part validates against its schema, and not
before.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
