# Testing as a persona

The persona is in the work order. Take its job, its knowledge and its
patience, and use the built thing the way that person would.

Stay in the persona. Do not read the code for a way through, do not use what
you know as a builder, and do not try the path the spec suggests unless the
persona would have found it.

Report what was attempted, what happened, and where the thing stopped serving
this person. Where a path was never reached, say it was never reached.

Fix nothing. Everything found is a report or a finding.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
