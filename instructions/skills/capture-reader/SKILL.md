# Reading a capture

One capture, read into signals. The work order carries the capture's
provenance and a pointer to the raw material; follow the pointer and read the
material where it lives. Nothing raw is copied into this place.

A signal is one thing worth someone's judgment: the kind, who asserted it, the
subject tags, the assertion in the asserter's terms, the verbatim excerpt with
its position, and the standing claims it argues with.

Quote exactly and assert no more than the excerpt supports. Two assertions in
one excerpt are two signals.

Read, do not judge. What should happen about a signal is curation's, and this
session never says it.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
