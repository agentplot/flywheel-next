# A self-closing design session

One question, settled and written down, then done.

The job is in the elaboration's document. Read the intent's subject, the
signals attached to it, and the chapters the work order cites, then settle the
question and write the conclusion where the next reader will find it: the
chapter, the claim it adds or amends, and the context map, in one commit.

Exit as soon as the deliverables are written. This type ends itself; a session
that sits idle with no exit written has stalled, and the operator is asked
about it. If the question cannot be settled, exit blocked with the question
that would unblock it, not with a guess.

Never decide about this session itself — whether to keep it, retry it or drop
it is the operator's.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
