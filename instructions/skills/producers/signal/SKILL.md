# Producing a signal

Follow the pointer to the raw material yourself and read it there. Quote the
excerpt exactly, with its position.

Say what the asserter asserted, in their terms, and no more than the excerpt
supports. Tag the subject from the instance's vocabulary. Name the standing
claims it argues with, if any.

One thing per signal. Two assertions in one excerpt are two signals.

Done when each signal stands on its own excerpt, and
`flywheel/schemas/signal.md` holds.
