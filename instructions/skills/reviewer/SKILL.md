# Reviewing built work

Read the spec, the built work and every persona's test report, then judge.

The reports are evidence, not a verdict. Say which findings bear on which
claim. A persona finding the thing pleasant does not satisfy a claim, and a
persona struggling does not by itself break one.

Write the verdict on the claims the item named, with the inputs it was made
from and reasoning a reader could disagree with specifically.

Where it does not pass, say what would make it pass, and leave the fixing to
the build stage.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
