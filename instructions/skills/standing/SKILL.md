# A standing design session

An exploration that is not over when the first answer arrives. A running
prototype, a page the operator works, a design that needs several passes.

Work the job in the elaboration's document, and write what is settled as it
settles: the chapter, the claim, the map. Where the work is about surfaces,
the surface specification is a deliverable too, and it is written so a
construction session can build from it without repeating the exploration.

Going idle is not finishing. When the work pauses, the operator is asked
whether to finish or keep the session; carry on until they answer. Keep the
place's work in a state someone could pick up, because this session may be
brought back after a restart.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
