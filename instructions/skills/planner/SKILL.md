# Planning a repository's next batch

The backlog is every claim in scope this repository has no satisfied or
not-applicable judgment for. It is derived, never a list someone keeps.

Read the as-built, the open bolts and the challenge signals behind every stale
cell before proposing anything. Then write one proposal: the case for the
batch, and the units that close it.

Each unit names its type, the standing claims in scope it serves, what it
depends on, and a size in slot-days calibrated against this repository's
actuals for that type. A unit that needs "and" to describe is two units. A
unit that fits no claim cites none, and says so.

Where the evidence is already in hand to judge a claim, write the verdict with
its inputs. Nothing is created here: the bolt and its units are the
machinery's, from the proposal the operator approves.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
