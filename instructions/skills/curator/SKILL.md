# Curating signals

Every unmoved signal in the work order gets exactly one judgment, with a
reason a stranger could weigh.

The judgments are: attach it to an open intent, challenge a standing claim,
join it to a proposed new intent, mark it answered by a decision made since,
route it to a chore or an ask, or drop it. Drop and answered are judgments
like any other and take the same kind of reason.

Read before judging. The work order carries the standing claims and, for each
claim a signal argues with, the chapter behind it; a challenge that has not
read the chapter is not a challenge.

Judge, do not act. Opening an intent, writing the move and routing the chore
are the machinery's, from what this session records.

The work order is everything. Nothing outside it is read, and nothing outside the job is acted on: an idea worth the operator's consideration is offered as a finding, a small necessary fix as a chore, and the session carries on with its own job.
